Frequently asked questions

Info you may need

Where do you operate?
Our company provides services in Winnipeg, Manitoba, and surrounding areas. To book a visit, contact us via phone number or email.
What services do you provide?
HandyMax offers the following services: tiling (for bathroom, kitchen, fireplace, outdoor) and flooring (heated floor installation, LVT flooring, vinyl planks and laminate installation).
How much do tiling and flooring services in Winnipeg cost?
The cost of tiling services starts from $10 per sq.ft. The price for flooring service — from $2 per sq. ft. The total cost of work is calculated according to the type and compexity of your project.
How long does tile and floor installation take?
HandyMax offers reliable services always meeting the deadlines. The actual duration of work depends on the area's size and complexity. For example, regular shower or tap surround tiling takes around 2 workdays.
